<objective>
When the user moves the last open subtask of a story to a done-category status, pop up a modal offering to also move the parent story to done. Both transition entry points (Sprint Board and the issue-detail sidebar status field) use one shared hook and one dialog. The dialog always lets the user pick among the parent's done-category transitions, and it honours the resolution requirement through the existing resolution flow.

Purpose: The user no longer has to open the story separately to close it after finishing all of its subtasks.
Output: The new module `services/jira/parentCompletion.ts`, the new `routes/dashboard/CompleteParentDialog.tsx` (hook + dialog), the wiring in SprintBoardTab, FieldsSection and StatusPopover, and tests for each.
</objective>
