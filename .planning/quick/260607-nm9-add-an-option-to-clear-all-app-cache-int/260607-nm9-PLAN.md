<objective>
Add a "Clear all app cache" option to Settings → Advanced → Data. It clears the two real transient caches in Taskflow: the avatar cache (in-memory blob-URL map + the `avatar-cache.json` Tauri disk store) and the react-query client cache. Preference/data zustand stores are intentionally OUT of scope — they are already handled by the existing Reset section ("Reset preferences" / "Reset all").

Purpose: Give users a single, safe action to recover from stale or corrupted cached data (e.g. stale avatars, stale issue/MR query results) without nuking their settings or disconnecting their integrations.
Output: New `clearAvatarCache()` service export (with test) + a new confirmation-gated row in the Advanced > Data subsection.
</objective>
