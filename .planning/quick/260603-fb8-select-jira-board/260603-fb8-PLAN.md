<objective>
Let users pick which Jira scrum board a project uses, fixing the blind `values[0]` board pick that selects the wrong board (e.g. "Copy of Eshop Scrum Board" 6708 instead of "Eshop Scrum Board" 163). Add a per-project stored board id, route every sprint/backlog/active-sprint consumer through it, and surface a board picker in both the onboarding wizard and Settings -> Connections.

Purpose: Greenhopper/Agile sprint endpoints consume a `rapidViewId` (board id). When a project has multiple scrum boards, the app currently picks the wrong one with no way to override. Implements FB8-1..FB8-4.

Output: `listProjectBoards()` service, `jiraBoardIds` per-project store map, stored-board-aware `useBoardId`, a `boardId?` param on both `fetchActiveSprint` copies wired through its 3 direct callers, and a shared `<BoardPicker>` used by the wizard and settings.
</objective>
