<objective>
When the user confirms "Clear all" in the floating notification panel, close the panel once the
clear animation (staggered slide-out + row collapse) has finished.

Purpose: after Clear all there is nothing left to look at; the panel should get out of the way on
its own instead of leaving an empty ghost card behind.
Output: updated NotificationPopover.tsx + tests in NotificationPopover.test.tsx.

Scope decision (judgment, per orchestrator note): ONLY the Clear all action closes the panel.
Dismissing individual cards or stacks keeps the panel open even if that empties the list - the user
is working through items one by one and the empty state is the expected feedback there.
</objective>
