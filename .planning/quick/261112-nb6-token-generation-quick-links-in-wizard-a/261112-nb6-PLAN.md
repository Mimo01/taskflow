- Why the scope is `api` and not `read_api`: the release actions write via PUT milestone, PUT MR, POST branch and POST milestone, so read_api would give 403.
- GitLab cannot prefill expiry.
- Jira DC cannot prefill anything, and its PATs have no scopes.
- The `user_settings` path 404s on GitLab older than about 16.x, which is accepted.
