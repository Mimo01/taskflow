<objective>
On the Backlog view, add right-click context-menu items "Send to top" and "Send to bottom" to a story row. The action reranks the story to the first / last position **within its own section** — its current active/future sprint if it's a sprint story, or the backlog bucket if it's a backlog story — and never changes sprint membership.

Purpose: Give a keyboard/mouse-free-of-precision-drag way to jump a story to the edge of its section, reusing the exact same persistence + optimistic UX as drag-to-reorder.
Output: A pure `resolveSendToEdge` helper (+ unit tests), two new optional callback props plumbed `BacklogPage → VirtualizedBacklogTable → BacklogRow`, two new context-menu items, and a `handleSendToEdge` handler routed through the existing `rankMutation`.
</objective>
