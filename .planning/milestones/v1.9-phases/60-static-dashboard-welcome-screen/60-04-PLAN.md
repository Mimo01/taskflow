| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-60-11 | Information Disclosure | `jiraToken` in `useState` | mitigate | Token never rendered (no `{jiraToken}` in JSX); kept in local component state — same lifetime as the route mount; not persisted; D-16 enforces single point of PAT load |
