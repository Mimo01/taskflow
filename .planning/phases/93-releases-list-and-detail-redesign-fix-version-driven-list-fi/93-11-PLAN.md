  <what-built>
The Releases list was rebuilt to sketch 002 tab F5. Top to bottom:
- **Hero:** a dashboard-style header with ambient curves, "Releases", and the summary "N planned · N overdue · N without date".
- **Timeline graph:** uncarded, on a faint band, showing 3–8 weeks depending on window width.
  - Controls: range · +N later, a Today button, and ‹ ›.
  - One chip per release; only the next chip has a bar.
  - Filled axis dots, a TODAY marker, and no orange.
- **Agenda rail:**
  - date stamps and a grey rail with hollow ring dots (dark ring for next, red for overdue, dashed amber for undated);
  - a TODAY line and month dividers;
  - the next-release card with its rocket, pill, package, team and a single "N/M done" bar;
  - aligned columns: issues · team · timing;
  - pills only on key rows, and plain countdown text on planned rows ("No issues yet" on far ones).
- **Released panel:** muted, showing the 3 most recent, with "Show older releases".
  </what-built>
