**Change 1 — WikiRenderer.tsx: route external `<a>` clicks through `openUrl`.**

In `taskflow/src/routes/dashboard/WikiRenderer.tsx`:

This handles both Jira `[name|url]` patterns (which jira2md converts to `[name](url)` markdown) and bare URL autolinks. The `img` handler is unchanged (it routes through `AuthImage` + `ImageLightbox`).
