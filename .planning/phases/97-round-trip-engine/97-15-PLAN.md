<objective>
Close verification gap 2 (SC1, review CR-02). The emitter writes Jira backslash escapes (`\*`, `\_`, `\^`, `\~`, `\{`, `\[`, `\!`, ...). Jira honours them, but TaskFlow's renderer does not: jira2md still pairs the delimiters, and the leftover backslash turns the generated tag into literal text (`a \*b\* c` shows `a <strong>b</strong> c`). The fix is to teach `preprocessJiraMarkup` the Jira escape set before jira2md runs, the same way `\+` is handled today.

Purpose: SC1 says re-emitted text renders identically through WikiRenderer, and SAFE-06 says escaping survives. Today every typed markup character the emitter escapes shows up as garbage after save.
Output: neutraliser in preprocess.ts, a code-opener guard, an extended inline-code decoder in pipeline.ts, and renderer tests (one per character).
</objective>
