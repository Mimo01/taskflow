<objective>
Change the show-shortcuts hotkey from `?` to `mod+/` to fix layout-independent triggering.

Purpose: The `?` key is layout-dependent (Shift+/ on US QWERTY, different combos elsewhere) so it fails to fire on non-US keyboards. The user wants Cmd+key (Mac) / Ctrl+key (other) shortcuts. `mod+/` is the react-hotkeys-hook spelling that maps to Cmd+/ on macOS and Ctrl+/ on Windows/Linux — it fires reliably regardless of keyboard layout.
